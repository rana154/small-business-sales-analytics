# Small Business Sales Analytics – Business Insights

## 1. Project Objective

The objective of this project is to analyse café sales transactions and identify business performance trends, popular products, customer behaviour and revenue opportunities.

## 2. Dataset Overview

- Business type: Café / Restaurant
- Dataset: Fictional sample sales transactions
- Data period: January–February 2026
- Records: 50 transactions
- Currency: INR (₹)

## 3. Key Business Questions

1. Which products generate the highest revenue?
2. Which product categories perform best?
3. What are the monthly revenue trends?
4. Which payment methods are most frequently used?
5. How much does an average transaction generate?
6. Are there opportunities to improve repeat customer purchases?

## 4. Analysis Performed

- Revenue and order analysis
- Product performance analysis
- Category-level sales analysis
- Monthly revenue analysis
- Payment method analysis
- Customer spending analysis
- Data quality validation

## 5. Business Recommendations

- Focus marketing efforts on high-revenue products.
- Review low-performing products and consider menu improvements.
- Encourage repeat visits through customer loyalty programmes.
- Monitor monthly sales to identify seasonal trends.
- Use payment preferences to improve the customer checkout experience.

## 6. Project Limitations

This analysis uses fictional sample data and is intended for portfolio demonstration. The findings should not be interpreted as actual business performance.

## 7. Next Steps

- Execute SQL queries in MySQL.
- Expand the dataset with more realistic customer transactions.
- Develop Python-based data cleaning and analysis.
- Build an interactive Power BI dashboard.
